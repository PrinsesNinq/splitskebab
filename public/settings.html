Add user

Anyone with a Google account can create their own Splitskebab account; adding someone here creates it for them ahead of time. "No account" means they signed in but did not register yet ("Give access" registers them). "Remove" deletes an account, but they can register again: "Block" keeps someone out until you unblock them.

Assign groups

Drag someone from "Everyone" into a group to add them, or from one group to another to move them. The × on a chip removes them from that group; the pencil renames or deletes a group. Every change is saved straight away.
