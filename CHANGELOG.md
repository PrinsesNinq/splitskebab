# Changelog

All notable changes are kept here in Keep a Changelog format.

## [Unreleased]

- Meal calendar: plan meals for your house or group, tick who joins, add guests; others answer invitations themselves
- Recipes with grocery lists scaled to the number of people, your own recipes, and "what can I make" from what you have
- Allergies on your profile; recipes and meals that contain them are marked in red for everyone at the table
- Split the receipt total equally, see who owes whom, send payment requests with your Tikkie or bunq.me link
- English and Dutch; sign in with any Google account
- Friends: send and accept friend requests, invite friends from outside your house to a meal
- "What can I make" ranks by what you have (main ingredients count most, swaps like spaghetti for penne), never by the shortest list
- 102 built-in recipes in English and Dutch, with filters: quick, vegetarian, vegan, budget, big group
- Planning a meal: recipe and costs are clearly optional and can be added later; a Done button takes you back
- Fixed: someone added to a house now also sees the meals planned before they joined
- Several people can add costs to one meal (each their own); the split works out who pays whom, in as few payments as possible
- Fixed: the app no longer starts blank or with an empty calendar on a phone; data is kept on the device and shows at once
- Anyone with a Google account can create their own account; admins can block someone
- Saying you will not join a dinner can come with a reason, shown to everyone at the table
- Profile: change your name and photo; a ? explains how to get a payment link
- Friends: search people by name, and see friends of your friends
- Privacy: allergies, payment link and friend list are only visible to your housemates, people at your meals and friends
- Tap a name in Houses or Friends to see their profile; the app reloads by itself when a new version is live
- Houses and groups have admins; admins see analytics: spending per day, who paid, and on what
- Pick a colour theme on your profile
- Installable on phones: Android shows Install, iPhone explains Add to Home Screen
- Messages: chat with housemates, people from your meals and friends; send feedback to the team, admins answer from an inbox
- Push notifications, offered the first time you open the app and changed per device on your profile: new messages, meal invitations with a reminder the day before, and costs added to a meal you ate at
- On phones, opening Splitskebab in the browser asks you to install it on your home screen first (needed for notifications on an iPhone)
- Share button in the top bar (and on the Friends tab): send anyone an invitation with the link to Splitskebab
- Steadier on phones: no more white screens on a slow connection (a splash shows while starting), page switches load from the saved copy, and the bottom bar hides while you type instead of floating up the screen
